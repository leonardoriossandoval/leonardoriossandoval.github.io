03 / BSPD board

I redesigned the BSPD board.

The Brake System Plausibility Device is a small safety board the rules require. Hard braking alone, like here, is normal: the shutdown circuit stays closed.

03 / Power alone

Full power, brakes released.

Also normal. A BSPD only reacts when both signals are high at the same time.

03 / Both at once

Both for 500 ms: cut.

Hard braking while 5 kW or more reaches the motors for over half a second means something is wrong. The relay opens the shutdown circuit and stays latched until reset.
